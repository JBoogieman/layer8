# Code of conduct

Layer8 is a small, spare-time project, and everyone here is here to help admins have a better day. Keep it that way.

## Do

- Be kind and patient. Most people here are fixing something at work, often in a hurry.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Disagree about code and ideas, not about people.
- Help newcomers: everyone was new to ConfigMgr once.

## Don't

- Harass, insult or demean anyone, for any reason.
- Post anyone's private information: names, emails, passwords, recovery keys, or details from someone's environment they didn't share.
- Spam, advertise, or post things that have nothing to do with Layer8.

## If something's wrong

Use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment, issue or pull request (the ··· menu), or report the account to GitHub. For a security problem, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, lock threads, and block anyone who doesn't follow this, in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else Layer8 lives.
